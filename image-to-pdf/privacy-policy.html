Privacy Policy

Image to PDF Converter · Last updated July 9, 2026

In short: Image to PDF Converter creates every PDF entirely on your device. Your photos and documents are never uploaded, stored, viewed, or transmitted to any server. There are no accounts, no cloud sync, and no file history kept anywhere outside your phone.

1. Who we are

Image to PDF Converter ("the App") is developed and published by Formatly ("we", "us", "our"). This Privacy Policy explains what information the App interacts with, how it is handled, and what choices you have.

2. Your files are never uploaded

The App's core function — turning JPG, PNG, or HEIC photos into a PDF document — runs entirely using your device's own processor. No photo, scanned page, or file you select in the App is ever uploaded to a server, cloud service, or any third party. Every step — image selection, cropping, reordering, merging, and PDF creation — happens locally and offline, and the resulting PDF is saved directly to your device's storage.

We do not have access to, and cannot view, any photo or PDF you create using the App.

3. Information we do not collect

The App does not collect, store, or transmit:

  • The photos, scans, or documents you convert or combine into a PDF
  • The contents, file names, or metadata of any PDF you create
  • Your name, email address, or any account information (the App does not require account creation or sign-in)
  • Your precise location
  • Contacts, messages, or any other personal files on your device beyond what you explicitly select for use in the App

4. Permissions the App requests

To function, the App may request the following device permissions. Each is used strictly for the stated purpose and nothing else:

PermissionPurpose
Storage / Photos accessTo let you select JPG, PNG, or HEIC images to include in a PDF, and to save the finished PDF back to your device.
CameraOnly if you choose to capture a photo directly within the App (for example, photographing a document page to add to a PDF). The camera is never accessed without your explicit action.
Network accessUsed only to display advertisements and check for app updates — never used to transmit your photos or PDFs.

5. Advertising

The App is free to use and supported by advertising. We use the following advertising networks to display ads within the App:

  • Google AdMob
  • Meta Audience Network
  • AppLovin MAX

These networks may collect device identifiers (such as your Advertising ID), general device information (device model, operating system version, country/region, language), and approximate location (derived from IP address, not GPS) in order to serve and measure ads. This data is collected and processed by the advertising networks directly, under their own respective privacy policies, and is not shared with us in a way that identifies you personally.

You can review or opt out of personalized advertising through your device settings:

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization

For details on how each network handles data, see their respective policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Meta Privacy Policy
  • AppLovin Privacy Policy

6. In-app purchases and subscriptions

If you choose to upgrade to Pro, the purchase is processed entirely by Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your payment card details — these are handled directly by Google under their own privacy and security policies. We only receive confirmation of your subscription status (active, expired, or cancelled) in order to unlock Pro features on your device.

7. Analytics

We may use limited, aggregated analytics (such as Google Firebase Analytics) to understand general app usage patterns — for example, how many people use a particular feature, or whether the app crashes. This data is anonymized and aggregated; it is not linked to your identity or the content of your photos or PDFs.

8. Data retention

Since the App does not upload or store your files or personal data on any server, there is no file data for us to retain or delete. Photos and PDFs created with the App remain solely on your device, under your control, until you delete them yourself.

9. Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will address it promptly.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the App or applicable law. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
