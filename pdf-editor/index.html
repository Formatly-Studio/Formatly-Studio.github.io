What it does

Ten tools, one app

Every common PDF operation in a single install. No account required. No internet needed. Just open and go.

🔗
Merge PDFs

Combine any number of PDFs into one file. Reorder pages before merging with a simple drag.

✂️
Split PDF

Extract specific pages or ranges into separate files. Pick exactly what you need, leave the rest.

📦
Compress PDF

Shrink file sizes dramatically without visible quality loss — ideal for email and sharing.

🔒
Lock & Unlock

Password-protect sensitive documents or remove existing passwords from files you own.

🖼️
Images → PDF

Turn JPG, PNG, or WEBP photos into a clean PDF instantly. Great for scanning paper documents.

💧
Watermark

Add text or image watermarks to any PDF. Control opacity, position, size, and rotation.

🗜️
PDF → JPG

Export any PDF page as a high-quality JPG or PNG image. Pick individual pages or export the full document at once.

✏️
Edit PDF

A full annotation toolkit built into the app. Fill forms, sign, highlight, redact, stamp, draw, and more — all without leaving the app.

🔢
Page Numbers

Add customisable page numbers to any PDF. Control position, format, font, size, and starting number.

Simple by design

Three taps, done

No tutorial needed. The same workflow applies to every single tool.

1
Pick your tool

Open the app and tap the operation you need — Merge, Split, Compress, Lock, or any other.

2
Select your files

3
Export and go

Built different

Privacy isn't a feature.
It's the foundation.

Most PDF apps send your files to their servers. We built ours so they never can.

Your files never leave your phone.

PDF Tools was built without a backend on purpose. There are no servers processing your documents, no accounts storing your history, and no telemetry watching what you do. Every operation runs natively on your device — even without a network connection.

🚫
No internet required
Works fully offline. No Wi-Fi, no data, no problem.
🔐
No server uploads
Files are processed locally. Nothing is ever transmitted.
🙈
No accounts, no tracking
No sign-up. No analytics. No ad identifiers. Zero.
🗑️
No history stored
We don't log what you open, edit, or export. Ever.
